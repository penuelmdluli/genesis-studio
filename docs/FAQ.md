# Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. Why is my video stuck generating?

Video generation typically takes 1-5 minutes depending on the model and complexity. If your video has been stuck for more than 10 minutes, try refreshing the page -- your video will still be processing in the background. If it remains stuck after 15 minutes, the generation may have failed and you can retry without losing credits.

## 2. How do credits work?

Each video generation costs a set number of credits depending on the model and duration. Credits are included with your subscription plan and reset each billing cycle. You can see your remaining credits on your dashboard at any time.

## 3. Why was I charged?

Charges occur when your subscription renews (monthly or annually) or when you purchase additional credit packs. Check your billing page for a detailed breakdown of all charges. If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact our support team.

## 4. How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your account settings and click on "Billing." From there you can cancel your subscription at any time. You will retain access to your plan features until the end of your current billing period.

## 5. Why was my prompt blocked?

Prompts are filtered for safety to prevent generation of harmful, explicit, or misleading content. If your prompt was blocked, try rephrasing it to remove any potentially sensitive language. Our content policy is designed to keep the platform safe for everyone.

## 6. How do I connect my Facebook page?

Go to your account settings and select "Integrations." Click "Connect Facebook" and follow the login flow to grant access to your Facebook page. You will need to be an admin of the page you want to post to.

## 7. Why didn't my video post to Facebook?

The most common reasons are an expired Facebook token or insufficient page permissions. Go to your integrations settings and reconnect your Facebook account. Also ensure the video has finished generating before attempting to post.

## 8. How do I export my data?

You can request a full export of your personal data from your account settings under "Privacy." We will prepare a downloadable file containing your account information, generation history, and associated data. This is provided in line with your data protection rights.

## 9. How do I delete my account?

Go to your account settings and select "Delete Account." This will permanently remove your account, all generated videos, and personal data from our systems. This action cannot be undone, so please download any content you want to keep first.

## 10. What's POPIA / how is my data handled?

POPIA is South Africa's data protection law, similar to GDPR in Europe. We comply with both regulations, which means your data is stored securely, used only for providing our service, and you can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. See our privacy policy for full details.

## 11. What video formats are supported?

Generated videos are delivered in MP4 format, which is compatible with virtually all devices and social media platforms. If you need a different format, you can use any free video converter tool to change it after downloading.

## 12. How long does generation take?

Most videos are generated within 1-5 minutes. More complex prompts, longer durations, or high-demand periods may take slightly longer. You will receive a notification when your video is ready.

## 13. Can I use generated videos commercially?

Yes, videos generated on paid plans can be used for commercial purposes including social media content, marketing, and business presentations. You own the rights to the output generated from your prompts. Review our terms of service for full licensing details.

## 14. Why does my video look different from the prompt?

AI video generation interprets prompts creatively, so results may vary from what you imagined. Try being more specific with details like camera angle, lighting, colors, and subject descriptions. Shorter, clearer prompts tend to produce better results than very long ones.

## 15. How do I get more credits?

You can upgrade to a higher plan for more monthly credits, or purchase additional credit packs from your billing page. Credit packs are one-time purchases that do not expire and are used after your monthly credits run out.

## 16. What AI models are available?

We offer a selection of cutting-edge AI video generation models optimised for different styles and use cases. The available models are shown when you create a new video. Each model has different strengths, and you can experiment to find the one that best fits your needs.

## 17. Is there a free plan?

We offer a limited free tier that lets you try the platform with a small number of credits. This lets you test video generation before committing to a paid plan. Free credits reset periodically and some premium models may not be available on the free tier.

## 18. Can I upload my own images/videos?

Yes, depending on the model selected you can upload reference images to guide the AI generation. This is useful for creating videos in a specific style or featuring specific subjects. Supported upload formats include PNG, JPG, and WEBP.

## 19. How do I share my videos?

You can download any generated video and share it wherever you like. If you have connected your Facebook page, you can also post directly from the platform. Direct sharing to other social platforms may be added in future updates.

## 20. Who do I contact for support?

For any issues, questions, or feedback, reach out to us at our support email listed on the website. You can also use the in-app feedback button to report bugs or request features. We aim to respond to all support requests within 24 hours.
